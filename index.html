Handout 1

Google NotebookLM

Grounded Intelligence
  1. 1

    Step 1Sign In & Open

    • Open your browser.
    • Navigate to notebooklm.google.com.
    • Sign in using your corporate Google Workspace account.
  2. 2

    Step 2Create Notebook

    • Click + New Notebook.
    • Enter a clear title for your project (e.g., "FY26 Budget Variance Analysis").
  3. 3

    Step 3Upload Sources

    • Click Add + Sources.
    • Select files from Google Drive (Docs, Sheets), upload PDFs/text from your laptop, or paste URLs (up to 50 sources).
  4. 4

    Step 4Ask Questions

    • Type your question in the chat box at the bottom (e.g., "What caused travel expenses to increase in Q2?").
    • View cited source excerpts.
  5. 5

    Step 5Pin & Save Notes

    • Click the Pin icon on any answer to save it as a permanent note on your project canvas.
    • Click + Add Note to write your own.
  6. 6

    Step 6Audio Overview

    • Open the Notebook Guide panel on the right.
    • Click Generate now under Audio Overview to make a 2-host audio podcast summarizing your files.
    • Click Generate now under Slide Deck to make a PPT presentation summarizing your files.

Handout 2

Gemini Enterprise No-Code Agents

Workflow Builder
  1. 1

    Step 1Open Agent Designer

    • Sign in to Gemini Enterprise at business.gemini.google.
    • If it asks for Connect to Google Workspace apps?, click connect for all stores (Gmail, Gchat, Drive, Calendar) and click Continue.
    • In the left menu, click Agents.
    • Click + Create Agent to open Workflow Builder.
  2. 2

    Step 2Define Instructions

    • In the setup pane, describe the agent's job in plain English:
    Agent instructions
    You are the Executive Personal Assistant to the CFO.

1. When asked about financial performance or reports, search Google Drive and present key metrics, Latest reports in structured tables and answer as per that only.

2. Use Google Chat, Google Drive and Gmail to look up stakeholder context and discussions.

3. Propose professional email drafts with clear To, Cc, Subject, and summary content.

4. Never send emails directly—always ask for confirmation before saving as a draft in Gmail.
  3. 3

    Step 3Test in Preview

    • In the right-side Preview sandbox, type sample questions (e.g., "how was my last quater results").
    • Verify its reasoning and calculations.
  4. 4

    Step 4Create & Chat

    • Click Create.
    • When the pop-up comes up asking chat with the Agent, click that.
    • Start asking questions related to docs and files available in your Gmail & Google Drive.

Gemini Enterprise Agent Scheduler

Recurring, automated agent runs
  1. 1

    Step 1Open Target Agent

    • Open your published agent in edit mode in Gemini Enterprise.
      • Click Agents on the left side bar of business.gemini.google. Under the tab Your Agents, click the three dots on your created agent and click edit (pencil).
      • On the right-hand side panel of your agent window you will see three tabs:
        FlowSchedulePreview
    • Click the Schedule tab on the chat agent builder canvas.
  2. 2

    Step 2Set Time & Cadence

    • Click + Add schedule.
    • Select Repeat frequency: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
    • Choose the specific day (e.g., "minutes" or "Weekly on Monday").
    • Choose the run time (e.g., "15min", "06:30 AM").
  3. 3

    Step 3Enter Recurring Task

    • Type the exact prompt:
    Scheduled prompt
    Pull bank cash balances from master sheet, compare with weekly forecast, and summarize net cash position.
    • Click Update to activate.
    • Review scheduled runs in the Chats section.

Handout 4

Gemini Enterprise Deep Research

Autonomous, cited research reports
  1. 1

    Step 1Open Deep Research

    • Sign in to Gemini Enterprise (business.gemini.google).
    • In the app navigation panel (≡), select Deep Research (or pin it from Agents).
  2. 2

    Step 2Enter Research Question

    • Type your research-related prompt:
    Deep Research prompt
    Act as a strategic advisor to a CFO preparing the FY2027 plan. As of November 2026, produce a deep-research report titled:

"Agentic AI in Finance: Separating Real ROI from Hype — A CFO Playbook for FY2027"

Research and cover:

1. CURRENT STATE (Nov 2026)
- Adoption rates of AI agents in finance (close, AP/AR, reconciliation, FP&A, treasury, tax)
- Latest CFO surveys on confidence, AI priorities, and budget shifts
- Failure/cancellation rates of agentic AI pilots and the root causes

2. ROI EVIDENCE
- Quantified benchmarks: close-cycle reduction, cost per transaction, forecast accuracy, DSO/working capital impact
- Separate vendor-claimed vs. independently validated results
- Full total cost of ownership: data infrastructure, agent execution/tool-call costs, human oversight, governance

3. MACRO & RISK CONTEXT
- Interest rates, inflation, demand outlook, geopolitical and supply chain risk
- Regulatory/tax pressure (e.g., Pillar Two, AI regulation, data sovereignty)
- Cybersecurity and AI-governance risks specific to finance agents

4. DECISION FRAMEWORK
- Which 3 finance workflows to automate first, and why (impact vs. risk vs. data readiness)
- Clear go/no-go criteria and ROI thresholds to scale a pilot
- 90-day, 6-month, and 12-month roadmap
- KPIs to report to the CEO and board

5. TALENT & OPERATING MODEL
- Skills the finance team needs, upskilling vs. hiring, and the junior "experience gap"

OUTPUT FORMAT:
- 1-page executive summary with the 5 most important recommendations up front
- Tables for benchmarks and the comparison of workflow priorities
- A "Risks & Mitigations" section
- A board-ready slide outline (5 slides)
- Cite every statistic with source and date; flag any data older than 12 months
    • Click Submit.
  3. 3

    Step 3Review Research Plan

    • Allow Deep Research to assess the prompt and generate an outline plan showing research steps.
    • Edit the plan to add or remove subtopics.
    • Click Start Research.
  4. 4

    Step 4Run Autonomous Search

    • Allow Deep Research to run the steps from the outline.
    • Stream real-time findings as they are generated.
    • View the displayed responses as they progress.
  5. 5

    Step 5Review & Export

    • Allow the system to complete processing and produce a detailed report with citations and a 2-minute Audio Summary.
    • Click Export to Google Docs to share with leadership.
